02 · Add capacity. Click run.

Bring your agents.
Put the fab to work.

Each teammate chooses how many coding agents to contribute from their laptop. Add cloud runners for more capacity. Together, they form the fab’s worker pool.

Click run. Available agents pick up the next eligible task, execute its predefined program, and return for more. Planning, building, testing, and triage happen across the layout.

03 · Operate by exception

The agents keep working.
You keep decisions moving.

Reviews, approvals, and questions arrive as notifications for the operator responsible. Route code reviews to owners, product questions to the product team, and releases to the on-call lead.

A gated task waits for its decision. Agents keep picking up other eligible work across the fab. Answer, approve, or request changes to move the waiting task forward.
